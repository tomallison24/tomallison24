Home Assistant

TokenSaved ••••••••

Everything that can get into Home Assistant as you: devices that joined through a share link (Home · …), other long-lived tokens, and sign-ins from the Home Assistant app or a browser. Remove one and it stops working at once. Home Assistant also alerts your phones and the tablet when a new one appears.

Send the link (Messages, AirDrop), then tell them the code separately. Both stop working after 5 minutes. The device gets its own token, named Home · its name. To remove one later: Home Assistant → your profile → Security → Long-lived access tokens.

It connects as you, with everything you can do.

On an iPhone, Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate settings. To use Home as an app, add it to your Home Screen first, open it, and paste this link into Address under the gear.

The address has to be https and reachable from your phone, such as your Nabu Casa address (in Home Assistant: Settings → Home Assistant Cloud, under Remote Access). A plain http:// local address is blocked by the browser, because this app is served over https.

Make a token in the Home Assistant app: ☰ → your name (bottom of the menu) → Security → Long-lived access tokens → Create token, then Copy: it's only shown once. It is kept on this phone only.

Sent a share link? Paste it into Address and tap Connect.
