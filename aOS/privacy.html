aOS

Privacy policy

aOS (AllisonOS) is a private set of apps made for one family. Last updated 3 November 2026.

Who it's for

aOS is used only by members of one family, each signed in with their own family account (a passkey). It isn't offered to the public, shows no ads and has no analytics or trackers.

Your Gmail

Some apps read your Gmail, only after you connect it yourself:

  • Travel, Orders, School and Money read it read-only (Google's gmail.readonly permission), to find bookings, orders, school news and spending.
  • Mail is an email app: it reads your mail and can label, archive and delete it, and manage your Gmail filters, when you ask it to.

What these apps read from your email is kept on your phone. It isn't stored on aOS's server, sold, or used for advertising.

If you choose to stay signed in to Gmail, aOS's server keeps the key Google gives for that (a refresh token), encrypted, for at most 30 days, and uses it only to give your own phone a fresh one-hour key. It's deleted when you disconnect Gmail, after 30 days, or when you leave the family.

The AI helper

Some features use Anthropic's Claude to read text for you: the AI helper's chat, and Orders, Travel and Money's checks of new emails. Before anything is sent, email addresses and phone, card, account and ID numbers are replaced with placeholders on your phone. Claude can be turned off app by app (Settings, AI helper); with it off, nothing from that app is sent.

What's kept on aOS's server

Your family account (your name and your passkey's public key), the settings you choose to have follow you between devices, what you share with the family (a shared calendar, lists, plans), and, if you stay signed in, your encrypted Gmail key. It runs on Cloudflare.

Google user data

aOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show you your own email in these apps and to do what you ask; it isn't transferred to others except as described above to provide those features, isn't used for advertising, and isn't read by people.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail in any app's Settings, or remove aOS's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Each app's Settings has Delete all data, which erases what it keeps on your phone.
  • The family's owner can remove an account, which deletes everything kept for it on the server.

Questions

Ask the family member who runs aOS (the owner of the family account).
