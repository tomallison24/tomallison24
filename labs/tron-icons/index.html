Tron icons

Five ways to draw the app icons in Tron. Trail frame was picked and is now Tron’s icons; Identity disc is how they were before. Each is made from the app’s own Vintage icon, so it works for every app, new ones too. Tap an app to see it big in all five.

On a phone the icons sit on your wallpaper and the phone rounds their corners, as here. Trail frame is the one in use.
